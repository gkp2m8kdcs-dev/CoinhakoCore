Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

In short: CoinhakoCore stores your portfolio data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal financial information.

1. Introduction

CoinhakoCore ("the App", "we", "us") is a dividend portfolio tracker developed for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored, and the choices you have. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data you enter

The App lets you record holdings, dividend payments, tax profiles, photos, and notes. All of this data is stored locally on your device using the iOS file system and standard app sandbox storage. We do not upload this data to any server controlled by us.

2.2 Data we do not collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email, or contact details (unless you email us directly).
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or tracking identifiers.
  • Location data, biometrics, or contacts.
  • Usage analytics, crash logs tied to your identity, or in-app behaviour tracking.

3. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect server-side data, the only "use" of your information is to display it back to you inside the App and to perform calculations on your device (such as yield, annualized income, and tax estimates).

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or social plugins. The App does not request remote market data, and therefore does not transmit your holdings to any third party. The only third parties involved are:

  • Apple App Store — for distribution and optional crash reporting you control in iOS Settings.
  • Apple's on-device frameworks (Foundation, SwiftUI) used to run the App locally.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your portfolio data lives in the App's sandboxed container on your iPhone or iPad. It is protected by the same iOS security model that protects your photos and messages. We recommend that you keep your device passcode enabled and your iOS version up to date.

If you enable iCloud or device backups through Apple, your App data may be included in encrypted device backups per Apple's policies. You can manage this in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Backup.

6. Your Choices and Controls

You can at any time:

  • Delete individual holdings, dividends, or photos from inside the App.
  • Erase all App data by deleting and reinstalling the App.
  • Disable iCloud backup of the App through iOS Settings.
  • Contact us to exercise any other privacy right available to you under applicable law.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. International Users

Because your data is stored on your device and not on our servers, there is no cross-border transfer of your portfolio data by us. If you contact us by email, your message will be processed on the email provider's infrastructure; please review your email provider's privacy policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect the change. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
