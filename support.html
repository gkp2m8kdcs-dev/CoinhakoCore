Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

All your holdings, dividends, photos, and notes live locally on your device inside the iOS app sandbox. Nothing is uploaded to our servers because we don't operate any. You can confirm this by using the App with airplane mode on — every feature still works.

How do I back up my portfolio?

Your data is included in your standard iOS device and iCloud backups, governed by Apple's policies. If you'd like a manual export, go to Settings → Data in the App and use the export option to save a JSON file. You can re-import it on another device.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Yes. Set your base currency in Settings → Currency. Each holding records its own native currency, and totals are converted using the exchange rates you configure in Settings → Rates.

How are tax estimates calculated?

The App uses your configured tax profile (withholding, foreign tax credit, filing status) to estimate net-of-tax income from recorded dividends. Estimates are informational and not a substitute for advice from a licensed tax professional.

How do I delete all my data?

Open Settings → Data → Erase All. This permanently removes every holding, dividend, photo, and setting from the device. The action cannot be undone, so export first if you want a copy.

Does CoinhakoCore connect to the internet?

No. The App makes no network requests for portfolio data. The only network access happens when you tap a link to our website or contact support by email.

Which iOS versions are supported?

CoinhakoCore requires iOS 16 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad.

How do I request a feature?

We love hearing from users. Email selmabarankolay@gmail.com with "Feature" in the subject line. Describe the workflow you'd like to see and, if possible, why it would help.

Tips to get the most out of CoinhakoCore

1 Log on payday

Record dividends the day they hit your account — small, regular entries keep your trends accurate and your tax profile up to date.

2 Use cost basis

Enter share count and average cost to unlock yield-on-cost and DRIP projections in the Insights tab.

3 Set your tax profile

Configure withholding and foreign tax credit once and every dividend estimate will respect it automatically.

4 Export regularly

Use the JSON export before major iOS upgrades or device changes so you always have a portable copy.
